Privacy Policy for Shubhavela / శుభవేళ

Effective Date: October 30, 2026  |  Developer: Shubhavela Team  |  Contact: shubhavela@investyum.com

1. Overview

Shubhavela (శుభవేళ) computes Telugu Panchangam calendars and stores family records and japa state locally on your device. Android includes Google Mobile Ads; the current iOS app does not include an advertising SDK.

2. Data Collection & Usage

A. Family Records and Japa State

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, sign in, or provide an email, phone number, or name to use Shubhavela.
  • Device-Local Spiritual Data: Reminders, family dates, notes, custom mantras, and japa progress are saved in private app storage. Shubhavela does not upload these records to an application server or provide cloud synchronization.
  • Backups: Android app backup is disabled. iOS app state may be included in operating-system backups according to your device settings.

B. Advertising & Usage Analytics (Google Mobile Ads & Firebase Analytics)

  • The Android app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements and may use Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) in production releases to measure anonymous app usage and ad performance.
  • Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics may collect pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or app instance ID), coarse IP-derived region, and aggregate app interaction events in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Strict Non-PII Boundary: Personal Family Vault entries (names, notes, birth/shraddha dates), custom mantra text, and raw GPS coordinates are never sent to analytics.

C. Location Data (GPS)

  • When you tap "Use my location", the app requests coarse location permission and reads a single position solely in memory to pick the nearest built-in city. The reading is not stored or transmitted to a Shubhavela server.
  • You can use the app fully without location permissions by choosing any city from the built-in offline directory.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) receive only standard advertising telemetry governed by their privacy policies.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Delete any individual reminder directly inside the Family Vault screen.
  • Permanently wipe all stored data anytime via Android Settings > Apps > Shubhavela > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Family records and japa state are handled locally regardless of age. Android advertising services may process the advertising telemetry described in Section 2B.
